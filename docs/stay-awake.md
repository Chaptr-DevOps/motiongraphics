# Try staying awake (StayAwake)

VO: `public/audio/stay-awake-vo.mp3` (ElevenLabs, Oct 29, 2026). The last word ends at 33.78 s.

Lying in bed, trying hard to fall asleep? Try staying awake instead.

It's a real insomnia technique called paradoxical intention.

Sleep is like a forgotten name: chase it, and it slips away. Trying to sleep puts your brain in effort mode, and effort keeps you alert. In a Glasgow study, two weeks of this made people worry less about sleep.

Next time, lie in the dark, eyes open, and tell yourself: stay awake a little longer.
