On-screen hook (first frame): Racing mind at bedtime? Move the meeting.

Hook: The moment you lie down, your brain calls a meeting. Here’s how to move it.

Transition: And the fix happens before bed.

Body: All day, your worries have nowhere to land. Then the house goes quiet, and they all ping at once, like an inbox you haven’t opened since morning. But in bed you can’t act on any of them, so your mind keeps them looping so nothing gets dropped.

Fix: So tonight, an hour before bed, take fifteen minutes. Draw two columns: what’s on your mind, and one small next step for each. Then fold the page. In one study, this quieted racing thoughts at bedtime. If a worry shows up later, tell it: you’re on the list.

On-screen source: Carney & Waters, 2006
