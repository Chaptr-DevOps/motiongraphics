# Picture a place (PictureAPlace)

VO: `public/audio/picture-a-place-vo.mp3` (ElevenLabs, Oct 16, 2026). Last word ends ~49.70 s.

Your mind won't switch off, and counting sheep is too boring to help.

Picturing a place you love works better.

Counting only fills a corner of your mind, so worries slip into the rest. But a rich scene, with sounds and smells, fills the whole room. In an Oxford study of people with insomnia, those who pictured a calm scene said they fell asleep faster than people told to just distract themselves.

It's called imagery distraction. Here's how it works. Pick a calm place you love, like a beach. Feel the warm sand under your feet. Hear the waves roll in. Smell the salt. Watch the light on the water. If a worry drifts in, don't fight it. Just walk back to the waves.
