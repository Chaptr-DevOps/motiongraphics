# Warm shower (WarmShower)

If you shower right before bed, you're about an hour too late.

You don't need a longer shower, just an earlier one.

To fall asleep, your body has to cool down.

Warm water sends heat out to your hands and feet,
which work like radiators and let it go.

But that cooling takes time. Straight to bed, you're still warm.

Researchers at UT Austin pooled 17 studies.

A warm shower one to two hours before bed helped people fall asleep about ten minutes faster.
