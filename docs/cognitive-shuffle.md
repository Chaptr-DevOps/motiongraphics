## Script

Your mind won't switch off? Don't empty it. Give it a word game.

It's called cognitive shuffling.

Trying to think of nothing leaves a gap, and worries fill it.

Pick a word, like "bread." For B, picture random things: a banana, a bike, a bear.

When you run out, move to R.

Keep them quick and unrelated, so nothing becomes a story.

It's meant to copy the jumbled pictures your brain makes as you drift off.
